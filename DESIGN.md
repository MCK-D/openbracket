# Foundation design

The tournament shell is an Operate surface: prioritize a readable tournament list and accessible schedule over decorative motion. The provisional identity uses warm white, dark green text and a forest green action color. Geist is self-hosted. Use one 6px control radius, 44px minimum controls, visible focus, and semantic tables with captions. Tailwind tokens live in src/styles/globals.css; shadcn components live in src/components/ui. The full visual identity and native design audit remain backlog work.
