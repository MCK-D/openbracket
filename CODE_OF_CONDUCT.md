# Code of conduct

We welcome respectful collaboration regardless of background, identity, experience, or disability. Be constructive, assume good intent, and make room for different needs and perspectives.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Keep feedback about the work specific and actionable.

Contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile to report concerns. Do not publish personal information in an issue. Maintainers may remove harmful content, ask for a change in behavior, or restrict participation. Reports should be handled confidentially and fairly.
